
# Shaar Logo Manifesto

> A logo should make people feel what Shaar promises.

## Core Emotion

"Estou feliz e seguro que terei uma experiência simplesmente foda."

## Emotional Hierarchy

1. Segurança
2. Confiança
3. Expectativa
4. Experiência
5. Memória

## Philosophy

Shaar does not sell access.
Shaar sells the feeling that something incredible is about to begin.

Technology exists only to disappear.

## Symbol Philosophy

The symbol must not represent a gate.
It must represent what a gate makes possible.

Communicate:
- Expectation
- Belonging
- Warmth
- Confidence
- Possibility

The passage should emerge through negative space.

## Design DNA

### Horizon (~40%)
- expectation
- optimism
- openness
- horizontality
- lightness

### Embrace (~60%)
- belonging
- safety
- welcoming curves
- trust
- generous negative space

### Threshold
Not a visual family.
An emergent property created by Horizon + Embrace.

## Manifesto

The Shaar symbol should:

- Feel inevitable.
- Be memorable.
- Feel human before technological.
- Invite instead of explain.
- Gain meaning over time.

## Success Criteria

- Works without the word Shaar.
- Can be sketched from memory.
- Recognizable at 16px.
- Needs no explanation.
- Improves with familiarity.
- Feels like the simplest possible solution.

## Physical Tests

Must work as:
- favicon
- app icon
- embroidery
- engraving
- stamp
- signage
- laser cut

## Forbidden

Avoid:
- literal gates
- tickets
- QR codes
- padlocks
- blockchain imagery
- cubes
- hexagons
- circuit traces
- pixel art
- obvious "S"

The brand must never feel like a crypto company.

## Exploration Rules

Explore geometry, not illustrations.

Families:
- Curves only
- Curves + negative space
- Curves + horizon line
- Curves + architectural proportion
- Single continuous stroke

## North Star

"Our goal is not to create the best logo for Shaar.

Our goal is to create a symbol that can be recognized without the word 'Shaar' fifteen years from now."
